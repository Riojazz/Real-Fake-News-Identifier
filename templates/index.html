Detection Pipeline

01

Text Sanitization

Removes HTML markups, hyperlinks, digits, punctuation, and converts all characters to lowercase for uniform tokenization.

02

TF-IDF Mapping

Evaluates how terms appear in the text compared to the corpus to determine relative word frequency and penalize common fillers.

03

Logistic Classifier

Processes the transformed feature coordinates through a trained sigmoid function to estimate classification probabilities.
